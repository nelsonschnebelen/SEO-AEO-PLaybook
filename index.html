05 · Features & plugins

Six jobs. That’s the whole stack.

What each one costs you, and what does it on your platform.

1

Let the AI crawlers in

Blocker 10 min Web person

Block OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot or ClaudeBot and you’re not ranked low — you’re missing. Security plugins do this by accident constantly.

Where: robots.txt — then your CDN or firewall, which blocks crawlers regardless of it.

2

Restaurant schema

20 min Plugin or paste

Your page says “5pm till late”. Schema says opens 17:00. Nothing left to interpret — which is what an agent needs before it acts.

Where: your SEO plugin, or one pasted JSON-LD block. Generate yours below.

3

A menu made of text

1–2 hrs Your website

The test: try to select a dish name with your cursor. Can’t? No assistant can read it — and Maps now sends diners hunting a specific dish.

Shortcut: photograph the printed menu, ask ChatGPT for clean HTML, check every price yourself.

4

Answer pages

Half a day Your website

Assistants lift passages, not pages. Question as the heading, answered in the first 40 words. 15–25 of them is the most quotable thing you can publish.

Say what AI can’t guess: price, reservation policy, biggest party, parking, transit, accessibility, kids, dress code, last seating.

5

A connected booking or ordering provider

2027 critical Your provider

The one that turns a mention into a Reserve button. If your tables live in a notebook, the assistant describes you and books someone else.

Ask your provider: are we live in ChatGPT and Maps? Tables: OpenTable, Resy, Yelp, SevenRooms. Orders: Toast, Olo, Square.

6

Reviews that name dishes

Ongoing In the shift

One review naming the short rib beats three saying “great!”. Whatever repeats becomes your description — good or bad.

Where: QR on the check, a line in the receipt email — five a shift is 150 a month. Never gate the ask, pay, or solicit on Yelp.

What does the job on your platform

WPWordPressRank Math or Yoast. Check the Restaurant type specifically.▸
  • Rank Math — widest schema coverage of the big three. Confirm it exposes the Restaurant subtype with menu, hours and price range.
  • Yoast — solid, Google-first. Fine if you already run it; its AI features are brand monitoring, not schema.
  • Can’t get the fields you need? Stop fighting the plugin and paste the markup. Generator below.
  • Watch for: security plugins blocking AI crawlers. The most common own-goal on WordPress.
SHShopifyAgent-ready already. Your job is restaurant schema, not product schema.▸
  • Agentic storefronts and llms.txt are done for you.
  • Shopify thinks in Product. You need Restaurant — usually a JSON-LD block in theme.liquid.
  • Your menu is probably a collection or a PDF. Build a real text menu page.
SQSquarespace, Wix & buildersBasics built in. Schema needs a code block.▸
  • Restaurant schema needs a code injection block in site-wide header settings.
  • Check your menu block renders text, not an image. Several builders ship menu widgets that output images or embedded PDFs — invisible to every assistant.
  • Confirm the builder isn’t blocking AI crawlers on your behalf.
</>Custom or agency buildEasiest to get right. Most often blocked by a rule nobody remembers.▸
  • Check robots.txt and your CDN, WAF and bot protection separately. Bot managers block unfamiliar agents by default, and AI crawlers are unfamiliar.
  • If the site renders only in JavaScript, verify the facts are in the raw HTML. Assume the crawler doesn’t run your app.
POSToast, Olo or a provider-hosted siteBooking comes free. Schema control comes last.▸
  • Upside: your provider does the assistant integrations. Job 5 may already be done — ask them.
  • Catch: you usually can’t touch markup or robots.txt. Keep your own domain as the source of truth and point your Google listing at it, never at a delivery app taking a cut.

Five things to skip

× Paying for llms.txt Log studies found AI crawlers almost never request it — 0.1% of AI bot visits in one 90-day test. Google says Search doesn’t use it; OpenAI still points to robots.txt. Free from your platform? Fine. Don’t buy it.
× “AEO tools” promising AI rankings Nobody controls who gets named. Do the six jobs first, for free.
× Marking up your own star rating A policy violation and a common cause of penalties. Let the review platforms carry the stars.
× AI-written allergen info Never publish it without your kitchen checking line by line. The one place a confident wrong answer hurts someone.
× Bought links and directory blasts Plus keyword-stuffing your business name, which breaks Google’s rules outright. None of it moves an assistant.

07 · The plan

90 days. Readable, quotable, bookable.

Run your score first and this rebuilds around your own gaps.

Days 1–30

Get readable

Let them in, then give them something to read.

  • Unblock OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot
  • Check your CDN and firewall separately
  • Claim your Google listing
  • Hours plus a year of holidays
  • Specific category, price range, every attribute
  • Menu out of the PDF
Days 31–60

Get quotable

Build what gets you named.

  • 15–25 diner questions, answered in 40 words
  • Parking, transit, accessibility on one page
  • Price, reservations, dietary in plain text
  • Restaurant schema on homepage and menu
  • Claim Apple Business Connect and Bing Places
  • Reconcile every source to your own site
Days 61–90

Get bookable

Start now — it depends on other people.

  • Ask your booking provider: are we live in ChatGPT and Maps?
  • Same question to your ordering provider
  • Claim every source AI cites for your city
  • Pitch two local writers
  • Connect visits at the POS so you can see it working
  • Re-run your score and compare
